Projects

Some of the projects I have worked on, both in school and in my free time.

TIPE – Music genre classification

Project carried out during my preparatory classes and presented at the TIPE oral exam. The goal: automatically recognize the genre of a music clip using machine learning, based on the GTZAN dataset. I coded my own versions of the classification models from scratch (k-NN, SVM and Random Forest) and compared them with the ready-made implementations from the scikit-learn Python library. This gave me both a deep understanding of how these algorithms work and practical skills with scikit-learn. I presented this work with a Beamer/LaTeX slideshow.
